Terms of service

The commissioner refers to the person requesting the drawing (you).
The artist refers to the person fulfilling the request (me).

No changes to the terms will be accepted.

Introduction

The commissioner undertakes to act as an adult or an emancipated minor. The artist will not accept any requests from a non-emancipated minor.

The artist undertakes to communicate regularly with the commissioner and to respond within a reasonable timeframe.

The artist agrees to draw humans, humanoids, anthropomorphics, animals, and plants. The artist will not draw "gore" content. The artist may draw sexual content (suggestive or explicit).


Delivery for digital piece

The artist agrees to deliver the piece within a reasonable time. Written turnaround times are estimates. If the artist knows they will need more time, they will notify the commissioner.

In the end, the commissioner will receive an image file (.png) or animation file (.gif). If requested, the artist can send a speed paint (time-lapse).

Once the commission is completed, the commissioner may leave a review.


Delivery for traditional piece

The artist agrees to deliver the piece within a reasonable time. Written turnaround times are estimates. If the artist knows they will need more time, they will notify the commissioner.

Once the commission is complete, the commissioner will receive a photograph of the artwork.

Postal deliveries to France and Belgium are possible. The cost of postage is payable by the commissioner, and payment must be sent to the artist via PayPal once the artwork is complete and before it is posted. No refunds will be issued. Postage times are not included in the stated deadlines.

Please let the artist know right at the start if you’d like a physical delivery (if you change your mind, that’s no problem)!

Once the commission is completed, the commissioner may leave a review.


Payment

The prices listed above are minimum prices; they have been set deliberately low to encourage as many people as possible to donate. If you can afford to, please do not hesitate to give more to help those in need. The main aim is to do a good deed, and the artist will be free to acknowledge the generosity of their patrons as they see fit.

The amount requested by the artist can be split between different charities.

The donation must be made and proof of payment provided before the artist begins drawing. A simple screenshot showing the recipient of the donation, the date of the donation and the amount will suffice as proof of payment.

The donation must have been made in connection with the current art request. It cannot be reused as proof of payment for a subsequent request. Similarly, donations made prior to the art request will not be taken into account. The donation serves as a means of payment to the artist.

As the money does not come into my possession, no refunds can be issued!


Usage

The commissioned art cannot be used for commercial purposes or any profit-generating activity, including advertising, marketing, reselling, or merchandising. The commissioned art is for personal use only.

The commissioned piece cannot be used to train any kind of algorithm, including artificial intelligence, or to create NFTs without the written consent of the artist.

The artist may post their work on social media to promote their activity. The commissioner may request to keep the art private for a certain period (e.g., if the piece is a gift).

The commissioner may repost the completed work wherever they wish, provided that the platform does not run or use generative AI on its content, and that the artist is properly credited (with a link!).


Piece edits

The commissioner may enquire about the artist’s progress on the commission at any time. The commissioner may then request changes or modifications. The commissioner may request two rounds of changes per stage (composition – sketch – line art – colour – shading/rendering) for works classified as 'clean'. For works classified as ‘sketches’, the commissioner may request minor changes to the final piece. It is up to the commissioner to be specific in their request.
Major edits (composition changes) cannot be made after the sketch is approved.

The artist will always send a sketch to the commissioner for approval (unless the commission is for a sketch). They may also send other stages of their work at the commissioner’s request or if they deem it necessary.

If the commissioner does not respond to a progress update within three days, it will be deemed approved.

Once the final product has been approved by the commissioner, the artist will not make any further edits. The commissioner is not permitted to edit the artwork, draw over it or alter it in any way. Only resizing and compression are permitted.

If you would like to make any further changes later to your artwork (such as applying a filter or adding a transparent background, etc.), you are welcome to contact the artist again. If the artist still has the file, any necessary edits may be carried out free of charge.
